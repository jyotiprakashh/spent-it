## Long description

**SpentIt is the expense tracker for people who don't want their money habits in someone else's database.**

- 100% offline. No login, no signup, no sync.
- Encrypted by default. AES-256-encrypted SQLite on every read and write.
- Biometric lock. Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.
- Beautiful, fast UI. 60fps lists with thousands of transactions.
- Insightful charts. Spending donut, daily trend, 6-month bars, year-to-date area.
- Multi-account. Cash, bank, credit card, savings, investments.
- Transfers between accounts. Atomic, doesn't pollute your spending analytics.
- Budgets. Monthly per category + overall. Amber and red banners when you cross 80% and 100%.
- Categories. Tap a category in the donut to drill into a filtered list.
- Backup. Create an encrypted file you fully control. Restore on a new device any time.

**Why?**

Most expense apps sell your data, or want you to. SpentIt doesn't connect to the internet at all. The Android build has no `INTERNET` permission. The iOS build has no networking entitlements.

This is a finance app that respects your finances.
